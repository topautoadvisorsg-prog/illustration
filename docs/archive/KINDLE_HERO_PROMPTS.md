## LOCKED STYLE PREAMBLE  *(prepended to every render — pulled from the codebase's Illustration DNA)*
> A 19th-century naturalist field-guide illustration in the tradition of hand-colored
> botanical and zoological plates — confident, expressive pen-and-ink linework in warm
> **sepia ink (#543C24)** finished with **rich, saturated watercolor and gouache**, like a
> freshly hand-colored vintage chromolithograph plate. Reference: Audubon, Ernest Thompson
> Seton, Redouté. **Color is bold, luminous, and vivid — never faded, muted, pale, neon, or
> digital.** Anatomically and botanically accurate to field-guide standard (habitat, bark,
> gill, venation, proportion). Light is warm, soft, and directional, as if from a high
> window in an autumn study. Painted on **aged fibrous parchment (#E0C8A0)** with subtle
> patina; whites are the parchment itself, never bright paper-white; soft feathered wash
> edges. Mood: contemplative, reverent, expedition-journal. **Full-bleed single hero
> subject. Museum-grade — a print someone would frame.**

## LOCKED NEGATIVES  *(every render)*
> NO text, letters, words, numbers, labels, captions, titles, signatures, watermarks, or
> borders of any kind. No photography or photorealism; no 3D render, flat vector, isometric,
> low-poly, anime, manga, cartoon, or comic линework; no anthropomorphized animals or
> cartoon expressions; no modern/man-made objects unless explicitly named; no maps/UI/
> infographic styling.

## Chapter modifiers  *(appended per chapter — the chapter's visual identity)*
- **Region:** wide, high vantage, big transitional skies; awe with a thread of danger.
- **Animals:** dynamic and alive — low angle for power / eye-level for intimacy / motion; golden-hour & dawn-dusk drama.
- **Plants:** intimate botanical framing, dewy backlight; the toxic run turns cold and ominous.
- **Trees:** look UP — grandeur, age, atmosphere (mist, shafts, storm sky).
- **Fungi:** low, ground-level, shallow focus, emerging from shadow; the deadly run is cold and spectral.
- **Terrain:** grand vistas, trails leading the eye, human-against-landscape, weather as character.
- **Survival:** practical and human — hands and action, working light, dusk/firelight.
- **Bushcraft:** warm, handcrafted, inviting — firelit, amber, intimate.
