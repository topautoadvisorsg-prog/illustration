# CHAPTER 5
## The Valley of Giant Flowers

Tessa had a piece of paper folded up small in her hand.

"What is it?" said Sivi.

"A drawing."

"Can I see it?"

"Not yet," said Tessa.

Then the cream pack lit up in the next room, and Sivi forgot all about it.

On the wall was a valley full of flowers, every one of them wide open.

Except one.

Out came the hoods and the boots and the wings. On went the packs.

The flowers were the size of houses.

Stems went up like trees. Dew hung over the children's heads in great glass balls, and the light came through them and landed on everybody's faces in wobbling circles.

Nico sat down in the grass to look up. The grass was over his head.

Zinumi got inside one of the dew balls and had to be got back out again.

Everybody in the valley was waiting for tonight.

Tonight was the night the flowers all opened together. It happened once every hundred years.

Little creatures were putting out lanterns. Somebody was practicing a song, badly.

The moon came up over the ridge.

And every flower in the whole valley opened at the same time, like a hundred slow gold explosions.

Except one.

"OPEN UP!" everybody shouted.

They sang at it. They shone lanterns at it. Somebody very gently pulled on a petal and got told off by somebody else.

Then the band came, and played the song at it, and when that didn't work they played it louder.

The flower shut a little bit tighter.

Tessa could not stop looking at it.

She wanted to know. She wanted to know so much that her hands went up before she thought about it.

The light went into the petals.

And there, curled up in the middle of the flower, was a small shape with its arms around itself.

"THERE'S SOMETHING IN THERE!" everybody screamed.

They ran at it from everywhere. They climbed the stem. They shouted, *hello, hello, come out, let's see you.*

The petals pulled in tighter than before.

Tessa stood very still.

"It was hiding," she said.

"And I showed everybody where."

Then she turned around and started pushing everybody backward.

"Back up. Back up. All of you, back up — *further* — no, keep going—"

Some of them had to be asked four times.

Bram made a wide warm ring on the grass, and everybody sat down behind it.

Nico found the thing that had been rattling all night — a dry pod, knocking against a stem — and held it still.

Sivi carried one small lantern up to the very top of the flower and left it there, and came straight back down.

Then Tessa went right up close, and said the only thing she could think of.

"We'll be over there."

And she went and sat down with everybody else, and the whole valley was quiet, and nothing happened for a long time.

Then one petal moved.

It opened all at once.

Light came out of it and went up the valley and set off every other flower a second time, and standing in the middle of it, blinking, was something small and shining that had never been outside before.

It looked around at everybody looking back.

Off came the hoods and the boots and the wings. Back went the packs.

Then Tessa took the folded paper out of her pocket.

She thought about it for a second.

And she opened it up and let them look.
